About

I am a research scientist at the French National Centre for Scientific Research (CNRS), in the Lattice lab (Langues, Textes, Traitements informatiques, Cognition). My work lies at the interface between computational literary studies / digital humanities and natural language processing (NLP), with applications in linguistics and psycholinguistics.

I hold a PhD in computational linguistics from Université Paris Diderot (Sorbonne Paris Cité), defended in 2018, on Cognitive Computational Models of Pronoun Resolution (supervised by Pascal Amsili and Adeline Nazarenko).

Research

My research interests, in a nutshell:

  • Computational literary studies and digital humanities — large-scale computational analysis of literary corpora and cultural data
  • Coreference and anaphora resolution, including with large language models
  • Winograd schemas and commonsense reasoning
  • Multilingual NLP and low-resource languages
  • Computational psycholinguistics
